A modern unisex salon for all ages — ladies and gents, students and seniors, walk-ins and regulars who’ve been booking with the same chair for years.

We’re on Park Road in the Shelthorpe corner of Loughborough — easy parking on site, kettle always on, and three stylists who actually listen before they pick up the scissors.

Whether you’re after a sharp gent’s cut, a colour you’ve been thinking about for a month, or a small transformation you’ll regret leaving any later — book a chair. We’ll keep the chat warm and the work careful.

“Friendly, professional, and a pleasant visit in a relaxing atmosphere” — what our regulars keep telling us.
